# Model and measurement card

DocMind retrieves document passages and shows eligible unverified drafts with deterministic quoted-source locations; hard evidence failures require human review. It is an experimental document QA service, with English controls and a measured-language gate. Hindi support is a measurement objective, not a validated general capability. French remains gated.

The historical English stack uses pinned BGE-small, MS MARCO MiniLM reranking and an English MNLI entailment classifier. The English/Hindi default uses pinned multilingual E5-small with required query/passage prefixes and dense retrieval. mMARCO MiniLM reranking remains an explicit measured alternative. The English NLI judge is an optional diagnostic, off by default and outside new draft release decisions; multilingual MiniLM NLI improves the Hindi component but reduces English recall and is not a blanket replacement. Hindi still requires review. The local-reader candidate is pinned Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 in FP16 with deterministic decoding. Exact revisions are recorded in the runner and per-run manifests. Component measurements support the retrieval default; the hosted generator remains unmeasured.

The multilingual NLI model was trained on XNLI development data. Independent component measurements exclude that split. XNLI sentence-pair performance does not establish claim-versus-PDF attribution, especially in Hindi. A blinded 15-item Hindi public-label sample has one reviewer; 11 items received decisive labels and four remained unclear. The full component cohort is not independently verified. See [release evidence](RELEASE_EVIDENCE.md).

The diagnostic PDF pool uses fixed templates over explicit source spans. Proposed answers and exact location checks remain synthetic drafts. Human question validity, answer correctness and claim support are separate. A final balanced cohort must be selected from conservative pilot timings before inspecting outcomes. No balanced PDF cohort was frozen in the current release. Failures and unserved rows remain in the requested denominator.

Historical reports describe their own dependencies, inputs and task restrictions. They are immutable and do not validate changed tokenization, Transformers versions or inference settings. New CPU/CUDA locks and representation identities require fresh baselines. ANN overlap against exact neighbors measures index fidelity, not semantic QA quality.

The project code license does not grant commercial clearance for all model training data. XNLI has noncommercial terms. mMARCO declares Apache-2.0 while upstream MS MARCO specifies noncommercial research use. The model cards, upstream datasets and individual PDFs must each be checked. Restricted raw text, translations and human annotation files stay outside Git until redistribution rights are established.

The initial 90-minute PDF/review allowance was expanded by user authorization to 150 minutes; public-label review retains its separate 30-minute cap. The original English PDF controls receive automatic checks only. The completed 27-item English review study has censored item timings and does not establish review-time savings. The newer own-document profile completed 17 PDF questions plus two paired DOCX tasks, with PDF candidate exact match 2/17 and five auxiliary-field format failures. Source questions were human confirmed. A separate blinded single-reviewer check judged all 17 baseline PDF answers: 10 correct, two incorrect and five unclear, with three correct answers held and two incorrect drafts. Of 25 extracted claims, 22 were supported. This selected cohort is not independent replication or general safety validation. Its additive parser replay is post-hoc and leaves historical results and the blinded baseline packet unchanged; neither study relabels the original controls. Fewer than 25 reviewed candidate releases yields insufficient evidence about false-release risk. Exploratory claim labels cannot validate general Hindi attribution or claim-extraction recall. Single-reviewer consistency is not inter-annotator agreement.

See [release gates](RELEASES.md), [evaluation protocol](EVALUATION.md) and [threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md). Missing measurements stay explicitly unmeasured.


The additive quality replay received a separate review of changed inputs. Its first-review current cohort has fourteen correct and three incorrect answers among seventeen. A [partial blinded second review](../reports/releases/r7/README.md) covers sixteen identical answers and 33 claim units: raw agreement is 87.5%/90.9%, with kappa 0.549/0.468 and nominal cluster intervals [0, 1]. Two answer judgments and three claim judgments are disputed; one answer lacks a completed second judgment. Independence is self-reported. Initial ratings remain unchanged and no consensus gold or validated automatic-release risk is asserted. The language-v4 sampler repair is separate from those frozen model outputs; cached v3 metadata remains readable until explicit reprocessing.
